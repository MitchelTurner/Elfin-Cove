Before you fuel

  • Have your vessel name and payment ready. Cards accepted (confirm with operator).
  • Fresh water is available at the float.
  • Do not leave your boat unattended on the fuel float.

On the live site, the dock operator texts a short line to the site's number and this section updates. Try closed weather or diesel 6.75.

Harbor & moorage

There are two places to tie up, both first come, first served: the float in the outer cove and the inner harbor basin, reached through a narrow channel at the head of the cove. The state built the harbor facilities in stages between 1956 and 2016, and the community operates them.

    Arrival checklists

    Arriving by boat
    1. Check the tide and plan your pass transit near slack water.
    2. Monitor VHF Channel 16 and hail the fuel dock or harbor before entering.
    3. Enter slowly between the red and green markers. The entrance is narrow.
    4. Fuel at the outer float first, then move to transient moorage.
    5. Let the harbor contact know your vessel name and length of stay.
    Arriving by floatplane
    1. Confirm your flight with the carrier the day before. Schedules change with weather.
    2. Pack soft bags and stay within the carrier's weight limits.
    3. Wait back from the float edge until the pilot waves you over.
    4. Arrange a cart or pickup with your lodge in advance.
    Trash, recycling and waste oil

    There is no road to haul waste. Pack out what you bring. Ask at the fuel dock about waste oil and recycling drop-off.

    Hydropower tracker

    1. Jul 2016The Utility Commission reapplies for rights at Crooked Creek and Jim's Lake, southeast of town. The project would supply about 90% of the town's electricity and replace roughly 22,000 gallons of diesel a year. Construction funding was not yet identified.
    2. Jan 2026A private applicant files for a federal preliminary permit for a 25 kW micro-hydro project near Elfin Cove, about 131 MWh a year, tied into the local grid. Public comments closed March 16, 2026. A preliminary permit allows study only, not construction.

    Emergency & health

    There is no clinic in Elfin Cove

    1. On the water: call the U.S. Coast Guard on VHF Channel 16.
    2. On shore: call 911 if you have a signal, and alert the volunteer EMS responders in town.Confirm phone coverage
    3. Medical evacuation is by air to Juneau or Sitka and depends on weather and daylight.
    4. Keep a basic first-aid kit and any prescription medicine with you. There is no pharmacy.

    The proposed Xunaa Borough

    Status: paused by court order

    A Superior Court stay in City of Pelican, et al. v. Local Boundary Commission has stopped the incorporation election while the appeal proceeds. Nothing changes for Elfin Cove until the court rules.

    Paused

    The City of Hoonah petitioned to form a new borough covering about 4,200 square miles of land and 6,100 square miles of water. It would include Hoonah, Game Creek, Elfin Cove, Funter Bay, and most of Glacier Bay National Park. Gustavus, Pelican and Tenakee Springs were left out. It would be Alaska's first new borough since Petersburg in 2013.

    1. 2023The City of Hoonah submits its borough petition to the state Local Boundary Commission.
    2. Aug 2024Commission staff recommend denying the petition.
    3. Nov 12, 2024The Commission approves the borough on a 3–2 vote. The written decision is issued December 20.
    4. Mar 2025The Commission rules on requests for reconsideration.
    5. Apr 2025A mail-only election is set for July 15, 2025. Voters would decide on incorporation, a proposed 1% seasonal sales tax, and the borough assembly and school board.
    6. Jun 13, 2025The Superior Court grants a stay. The Division of Elections pauses the election until further notice.

    What residents have said

    Elfin Cove residents filed public comments on both sides. These summaries are from the state's public record.

    In favor of including Elfin Cove

    Some residents argue a borough would bring elected oversight and a way to collect local revenue, which the community nonprofit cannot do.

    Against including Elfin Cove

    Other residents say the current community structure works, that Elfin Cove has more in common with Pelican and Gustavus, and that it should be left out as those towns were.

    Neighboring communities

    Pelican and others appealed the decision. Gustavus officials raised concerns about possible future taxes on offshore waters.

    Sources: Alaska Division of Elections · City of Hoonah petition page · Juneau Empire · KTOO · LBC public comment (Benton) · LBC public comment (Nemecek)

    History & nearby

    Timeline

    1. Tlingit nameX̱'óot'k'
    2. 1920sFishermen call the sheltered basin the "Gunkhole." Ernie Swanson opens a store, restaurant and dock.
    3. 1935Ruth Swanson requests a post office and names the town Elfin Cove.
    4. 1940sJohn Lowell arrives and builds more businesses. Fish buying and supplying the fleet drive the economy.
    5. 1956–1963Tidal grid, small-boat facilities and the inner harbor are built.
    6. 1981The community nonprofit is formed.
    7. 1983The seaplane float is built.
    8. 1998–99The school closes as enrollment drops.
    9. 2016Outer harbor improvements are completed.

    Year-round population

    U.S. Census counts

    1960 count not reported. Summer population is much higher.

    Climate

    About 104 inches of precipitation a year. Summer highs in the 50s and low 60s °F; winter temperatures mostly between the mid-20s and high 30s.

    Nearby

    Wildlife
    Inian Islands
    Strong currents and upwelling draw sea otters, Steller sea lions, harbor seals and seabirds.
    WWII site
    George Island
    A forest trail leads to an 18-ton, 6-inch naval gun emplaced in August 1942.
    National park
    Glacier Bay
    Across Icy Strait. Most of the park lies inside the proposed borough.
    Fishing
    Outer coast
    Close to the Gulf of Alaska, a major summer sport and commercial fishing ground.
